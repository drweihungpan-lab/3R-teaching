## Session 0: required setup before Session 1

Session 0 is a self-paced pre-course step with no classroom hours. Students follow the [Session 0 student guide](../docs/session-0.md) and post a check-in comment; the [Session 0 instructor notes](instructor/session-0.md) cover the sandbox, the check-in issue, the student message and common failures. The decision is recorded in the [ADR](../docs/adr/0001-wsl-and-ssh-for-all-students.md).

[Slide maintenance](slides/README.md) explains the converter and deck checks. Change the source and regenerate its deck together.

- [Pre-course survey](surveys/pre-course.md): experience, confidence, and setup readiness.
- [Post-course survey](surveys/post-course.md): matching questions and course feedback.
- [Final presentation and pass/fail checklist](assessment/final-presentation.md): all mandatory engineering evidence.

Surveys support course evaluation and are not graded.

Homework briefs are placeholders until the homework is defined.
