The inventory is five Markdown sources, five PowerPoint decks, four exercises, four homework briefs, and five instructor notes. [Slide maintenance](slides/README.md) explains the existing converter and deck checks. Change the source and regenerate its deck together.

- [Pre-course survey](surveys/pre-course.md): experience, confidence, and setup readiness.
- [Post-course survey](surveys/post-course.md): matching questions and course feedback.
- [Final presentation and pass/fail checklist](assessment/final-presentation.md): all mandatory engineering evidence.

The course serves 20 students in groups of three or four, with 15 classroom hours and approximately eight mandatory homework hours after Sessions 1–4, plus any guided practice not finished in class. Groups choose their own dataset and question without mandatory topic approval; modeling is optional. Homework prepares evidence for the final presentation. All mandatory requirements must pass there; there is no separate submission, resubmission, or reassessment. Surveys support course evaluation and are not graded.
