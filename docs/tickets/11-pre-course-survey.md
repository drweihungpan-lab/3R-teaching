Published as [GitHub issue #12](https://github.com/drweihungpan-lab/3R-teaching/issues/12), labeled `ready-for-agent`.

Approved course-authoring task. Implementation owner: the course author or an assisting agent. Student participation is not a completion condition.

## Parent

https://github.com/drweihungpan-lab/3R-teaching/issues/1

## What to build

Author a ready-to-use English survey for understanding the cohort's prior experience, confidence, and setup readiness.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Cover relevant experience and confidence with Python, Git/PRs, environments, packaging, APIs, testing/CI, and containers.
- [ ] Include short understanding questions about the course's three-R terminology.
- [ ] Include readiness questions for the tools and account access already required by the syllabus.
- [ ] Use clear response scales and stable question identifiers so relevant items can be repeated after the course.
- [ ] Explain the survey's purpose and that it is not graded; do not collect credentials or require a hosted survey service.
