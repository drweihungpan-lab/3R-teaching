- [ ] Cover Python, Git/PR review, virtual environments, packaging, APIs, tests/CI, containers, and the course definitions of the three Rs at an appropriate introductory level.

- [ ] Include post-course questions about workload, pacing, useful activities, and improvements.
- [ ] State in the survey introduction and administration notes that surveys support course evaluation and are not graded.
- [ ] Do not require a hosted survey service, automated data collection, or a new analytics application.
