## What to build

Author the student-facing final presentation brief and the instructor's mandatory engineering-evidence checklist.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Cover live Compose startup, CSV upload, API/package processing, visible results, service communication, and configuration hygiene.
- [ ] Require passing local tests/CI and input-validation evidence, repeated local outputs, and matching local/Docker outputs.
- [ ] Include repository history, meaningful PR/review evidence, and each student's authored PR and review of a teammate's PR.
- [ ] Use pass/fail with every requirement mandatory; do not grade statistical analysis correctness, model complexity, or UI polish.
- [ ] State that the final presentation is the only assessment, with no separate submission, resubmission, or reassessment.
- [ ] Provide flexible group time slots fitting three hours and groups of three or four, without hard-coding a group count.
- [ ] Deliver the checklist and presentation guidance only; Session 5 slides and instructor delivery notes are separate tasks.
