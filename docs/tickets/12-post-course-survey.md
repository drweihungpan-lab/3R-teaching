## What to build

Author the matching English post-course survey for evaluating learning and collecting course feedback.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Repeat relevant confidence and understanding items from the pre-course survey with the same scales and identifiers.
- [ ] Add prompts about workload, pacing, useful activities, remaining difficulties, and improvements.
- [ ] Provide brief guidance for comparing the paired questions without claiming confidence alone proves competence.
- [ ] State that the survey supports course evaluation and is outside pass/fail assessment.
- [ ] Deliver ready-to-use text without requiring collection infrastructure or an analytics application.
